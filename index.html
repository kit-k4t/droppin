Terms of Service & Privacy Policy
Last updated: June 3, 2026

🗺️ 1. What is DropPin?

DropPin is an anonymous, location-based platform where users can drop text pins on a shared global map.

🚀 Coming Soon:

  • Media Attachments — Photos and voice notes.
  • Private Groups — Invite-only or password-protected maps.

🔒 2. Anonymous by Design

DropPin does not require accounts, emails, or phone numbers.

A random User ID is generated and stored locally on your device. This ID is used to:

  • Identify pins you created
  • Prevent duplicate reactions
  • Maintain a consistent color marker for your pins

This ID is not linked to your real identity.

🌐 3. Location Access (Geolocation)

To provide a better mapping experience, DropPin uses your browser’s Geolocation API. When you allow location access:

  • Map Centering: Your coordinates are used only to center the map around your current area for relevance.
  • On-Demand Use: Your location is accessed only while you are actively using the app.

You may deny location access at any time through your browser settings. If denied, the map will default to a general global view.

📦 4. Data Collection & Storage

We only store data necessary for the core functionality of the platform:

  • Pin content — Text and coordinates
  • Anonymous User ID — Browser-generated identifier
  • Metadata — Timestamp and expiration settings
  • Reactions — Emoji counts linked to anonymous IDs

🚫 5. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • Names, emails, phone numbers, or social media profiles
  • Tracking cookies or browsing history
  • Continuous location tracking: We do not track or store your movements. Only manually dropped pin locations are recorded.
  • Media files (until future features are released)

⏰ 6. Content Lifespan & Deletion

You can choose how long your pins remain visible:

  • 1, 7, or 30 days — Pins are automatically deleted after expiration

To request early deletion, contact hello@droppin.app with your User ID and pin details.

🗑️ 7. Clearing Browser Data

  • Your User ID will be reset
  • A new anonymous ID will be generated
  • Existing pins will remain but will no longer be linked to you
  • Previous reactions will remain unchanged

⚠️ 8. Content Moderation & Safety

DropPin is a public platform. We may remove content that includes:

  • Harassment, hate speech, or bullying
  • Explicit or graphic content
  • Illegal content or personal information sharing

You are responsible for the content you post.

📋 9. Service Disclaimer

DropPin is provided "as-is" without guarantees of uninterrupted or error-free service.

📝 10. Changes to These Terms

We may update these terms from time to time. Continued use of DropPin means you accept the latest version.
