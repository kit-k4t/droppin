Terms of Service & Privacy Policy
Last updated: May 25, 2026

🗺️ 1. What is DropPin?

DropPin is an anonymous, location-based platform where users can place text pins on a shared global map.

🚀 Coming Soon:

  • Media Attachments — Photos, and voice notes.
  • Private Groups — Invite-only or password-protected maps.

🔒 2. Anonymous by Design

DropPin does not require accounts, emails, or phone numbers.

Your browser generates a random User ID stored locally on your device. This ID is used to:

  • Identify pins you created.
  • Prevent duplicate reactions.
  • Keep a consistent color marker for your pins.

This ID is not linked to your real identity.

📦 3. Data Collection & Storage

We only store data necessary to operate the shared map:

  • Pin content — Text notes and chosen coordinates.
  • Anonymous User ID — Your browser-generated UUID.
  • Metadata — Timestamps and expiration settings.
  • Reactions — Emoji counts linked to anonymous IDs.

🚫 4. Data We Do NOT Collect

We do not collect:

  • Names, emails, phone numbers, or social profiles.
  • Tracking cookies or browser histories.
  • Background location data.
  • Media files (until future media features launch).

⏰ 5. Content Lifespan & Deletion

You choose how long your pins remain visible:

  • 1, 7, or 30 days — Automatically deleted after expiration.

To request early deletion, contact hello@droppin.app with your User ID and pin details.

🗑️ 6. Clearing Browser Data

Clearing your browser data resets your local User ID.

  • A new anonymous ID will be created.
  • Your old pins will remain but will no longer display as yours.
  • Previous reactions will stay counted.

⚠️ 7. Content Moderation & Safety

DropPin is public. We may remove content that includes:

  • Harassment, hate speech, or bullying.
  • Explicit or graphic material.
  • Illegal content or personal information sharing.

You are responsible for the content you post.

📋 8. Service Disclaimer

DropPin is provided "as-is" without guarantees of uninterrupted service.

📝 9. Changes to These Terms

These terms may change over time. Continued use of DropPin means you accept the latest version.
